## Model 1: Broker Affiliate & RevShare Funnel (The #1 Most Lucrative Model)

In binary options (Pocket Option, Quotex, Olymp Trade) and CFD trading, the broker affiliate model is the single most profitable monetization mechanism.

### How Broker Affiliate Programs Work
Pocket Option offers an **Affiliate / Introducing Broker (IB)** program:
1. **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**: You earn **$50 to $150+** as soon as a user signs up through your link and deposits $50 or more.
2. **Revenue Share (RevShare)**: You earn **50% to 80%** of the net broker revenue generated by your referred traders for the entire lifetime of their accounts.
3. **Turnover / Volume Share**: You earn a percentage on every single trade placed by your referrals (win or loss).

### The "Unlock Free VIP Access" Funnel
Rather than asking beginners to pay $49/month out-of-pocket, you offer them **100% Free VIP Access**:
```
User Lands on SignalDesk
       │
       ▼
Views Free Demo (e.g. 5m EUR/USD only, limited to 5 signals/day)
       │
       ▼
Hits "Unlock Full 24/7 VIP Terminal (OTC Pairs + 1m Scalp + Live Countdown)"
       │
       ├── Option A: "Sign up on Pocket Option with our Partner Code & Deposit $50+" ──► YOU GET: $50–$100 CPA + RevShare
       │
       └── Option B: "Or Pay $39/month directly via Card / Crypto" ──────────────────► YOU GET: $39/mo SaaS MRR
```
> **Why this works:** 80%+ of users choose Option A because they need trading capital on Pocket Option anyway! You get paid by the broker, and the user gets the software for "free".

### Implementing the Broker Funnel in SignalDesk
1. **Banner & Buttons**: Add a glowing `"Claim Free VIP with Pocket Option"` button in the top navigation bar and modal.
2. **Affiliate Link Injection**: Place your affiliate referral link on all broker guide steps (`Step 1: Match Asset & Register`).
3. **Account Verification**: Users enter their Pocket Option User ID into a verification box on SignalDesk. Your backend checks with the affiliate API or manual CSV export to unlock their account.

## Model 2: SaaS Tiered Subscriptions (Freemium Model)

For users who already have broker accounts or prefer paying directly, offering tiered monthly/annual subscriptions creates recurring, predictable Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

### Tier Structure

#### 🆓 Tier 1: Free Starter (Lead Magnet)
- **Price:** $0 / month
- **Features:**
  - 1 Standard Asset: `EUR/USD` (5m timeframe only).
  - Standard signals (delayed by 30 seconds or capped at 5 signals per day).
  - Basic Moving Average indicators.
  - Access to Beginner Pocket Option Guide and Risk Calculator.
- **Purpose:** Collect email addresses, build trust, and showcase signal accuracy.

#### ⚡ Tier 2: Pro Trader (The Core Offering)
- **Price:** **$29 / month** or **$249 / year** (Save 28%)
- **Features:**
  - **All 31+ Assets**: Major Forex, Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL), Commodities (Gold, Silver, Oil), Indices.
  - **24/7 Pocket Option OTC Pairs**: Weekend trading on `EURUSD_OTC`, `GBPUSD_OTC`, `XAUUSD_OTC`, etc.
  - **All Timeframes**: 1m Fast Scalp, 5m Core, 15m Trend Wave.
  - **Live Real-Time Streaming**: Zero delay, sub-second polling, audio chimes.
  - **Exact Entry Zone & Real-Time Countdown Timer**.
  - **1,000-Candle Confluence Backtester** with parameter tweaking.

#### 👑 Tier 3: Elite VIP / Lifetime
- **Price:** **$79 / month** or **$399 Lifetime One-Time**
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Pro Trader.
  - **Automated Telegram VIP Signal Alerts**: Push notifications directly to their smartphone.
  - **Priority Access to New Assets & Indicators**.
  - **Exclusive Video Strategy Masterclasses** & weekly market analysis.
  - Direct 1-on-1 Discord support.

### Recommended Payment Gateways
- **Stripe**: Best for credit/debit card recurring billing (requires business entity).
- **Whop.com**: Designed specifically for trading communities, SaaS apps, and Discord/Telegram bots. Handles checkout, subscriptions, and access control out of the box.
- **Cryptocurrency (NowPayments / Coinbase Commerce / Cryptomus)**: Essential for global trading audiences where credit cards may have bank restrictions on binary options. Supports USDT (TRC-20), BTC, ETH, and Solana.
