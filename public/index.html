How to Trade with SignalDesk on Pocket Option QT (Quick Trading)

Welcome! If you're new to trading, don't worry. This step-by-step visual tutorial explains exactly how to take the BUY / SELL signals and Moving Averages from SignalDesk and execute them on Pocket Option to grow your capital with discipline.

💡
Golden Rule #1 Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your balance per trade.
VISUAL CHEATSHEET: SIGNALDESK vs POCKET OPTION QT See how each indicator maps directly to your Pocket Option screen
⚡ ON SIGNALDESK ANALYSIS
1. Signal Badge
BUY (Green) or SELL (Red)
Indicates direction predicted by Moving Averages & Confluence.
2. Recommended Entry Point
e.g. 1.12550 (Zone: 1.12546 - 1.12554)
The ideal price level to click your trade. Don't chase if it moves far away.
3. Suggested Expiry
e.g. 5m (5 Minutes)
Matches the timeframe of your chart (1m, 5m, or 15m).
4. Market Moving Averages
EMA 9 > 21 > 50 > 200 (Bullish)
Confirms trend health. Always trade in the direction of the EMA 200.
➔ EXECUTE
📱 ON POCKET OPTION QT BROKER
1. Select Instrument & Chart
EUR/USD (or same asset) on M5 chart
Always verify the payout is at least 80% to 92%.
2. Set Expiry Timer
Timer: 00:05:00 (5 Minutes)
In Pocket Option QT, set expiration to match the SignalDesk timeframe.
3. Enter Fixed Stake
Amount: $10.00 (1% of $1,000)
Use the SignalDesk Risk Calculator. Never guess your stake!
4. Click Action Button
▲ HIGHER / CALL If SignalDesk shows BUY
▼ LOWER / PUT If SignalDesk shows SELL
STEP 1

Match Asset & Timeframe

Open SignalDesk and Pocket Option side by side (or split your screen). Select the same asset (e.g., EUR/USD or BTC/USDT) on both platforms. Set your Pocket Option chart to candles and pick the matching timeframe (5m is ideal for beginners because it filters out random market noise).

💡 Pro Tip: Stick to pairs with high broker payouts (85%+). Never trade assets offering under 75% payout.
STEP 2

Inspect the Moving Averages

Before placing any trade, check the Market Moving Averages Dashboard on SignalDesk:

  • EMA 200 (Purple): The macro trend. If price is above EMA 200, only take BUY (HIGHER) signals.
  • EMA 9 & 21: Short-term momentum. EMA 9 crossing above EMA 21 gives the fastest bullish push.
  • EMA 50: Intermediate dynamic support/resistance where price often bounces.
STEP 3

Wait for the Exact Entry Point

When SignalDesk gives a BUY or SELL signal, notice the RECOMMENDED ENTRY POINT card. It shows the exact trigger price and the Optimal Entry Zone.

Wait for the price to touch the entry zone or the opening of the fresh candle. Do not click if the price has already shot 20 pips in that direction—patience is how professionals win!

STEP 4

Execute on Pocket Option QT

Once price is in your Entry Zone:

🟢 If Signal is BUY: Click the green "HIGHER" (CALL) button on Pocket Option. You win if price closes higher after 5 minutes.
🔴 If Signal is SELL: Click the red "LOWER" (PUT) button on Pocket Option. You win if price closes lower after 5 minutes.
STEP 5

Strict Risk & Discipline Rules

Trading is a marathon, not a sprint. Follow these non-negotiable rules:

  • Fixed Stake: Only risk 1% to 2% of your account balance per trade. (Use our Risk Calculator tab).
  • No Martingale: Never double your money after a loss! That is the fastest way to blow an account.
  • Circuit Breaker: If you lose 3 trades in a row, close Pocket Option immediately for the day. SignalDesk will show the red warning banner.
STEP 6

Log Trade in the Journal

After your trade completes, click "Log Trade at Entry Price" on SignalDesk. Mark it as WIN or LOSS. Reviewing your win rate weekly will make you a disciplined, profitable trader!

BROKER CONFIGURATION GUIDE

How to Setup Candles & Indicators on Pocket Option QT

Configure your Pocket Option chart to mirror SignalDesk's confluence engine with 100% precision. Choose your trading timeframe below to view the exact settings.

STEP 1: CONFIGURE CANDLES & TIMEFRAME
1
Chart Type:

Click the Chart Type icon (top toolbar) $\rightarrow$ Select "Japanese Candlesticks" (green/red candles).

2
Candle Timeframe:

Select M5 (5 Minutes). Toggle ON "Timer on Candles" to see seconds count down on the bar.

3
Expiration Order Timer:

In the right trade panel, set expiration to Timer: 00:05:00 (matches chart duration).

STEP 2: ADD INDICATORS ON POCKET OPTION

Click the Compass / Indicators icon in the top-left toolbar of Pocket Option and add these tools with the exact parameters:

EMA 9 (Fast Scalp) Moving Average
Type: EMA
Period: 9
Color: Cyan (2px width)

Shows immediate micro-trend direction. Fastest trigger for entry confluence.

EMA 21 (Pullback Dynamic) Moving Average
Type: EMA
Period: 21
Color: Amber / Orange (2px)

Your optimal entry pullback bounce line in trending markets.

EMA 50 (Mid-Trend Support) Moving Average
Type: EMA
Period: 50
Color: Sky Blue (2px)

Institutional pullback boundary where major swing continuations occur.

EMA 200 (Macro Baseline) Moving Average
Type: EMA
Period: 200
Color: Purple (3px width)

The golden trend filter: Price > EMA 200 $\rightarrow$ CALL only. Price < EMA 200 $\rightarrow$ PUT only.

RSI (Relative Strength) Oscillator
Period: 14
Overbought: 70
Oversold: 30

Filters exhaustion: Never BUY when RSI > 70. Never SELL when RSI < 30.

Bollinger Bands (20, 2) Volatility
Period: 20
Deviation: 2.0
MA Type: SMA

Detects compression squeezes before breakout momentum occurs.

AVOID THESE 5 COMMON BEGINNER TRAPS

Trading Against EMA 200

Taking a CALL when the price is far below the EMA 200 is swimming against a waterfall. Always follow the macro trend.

❌
Over-Trading (Revenge Trading)

Placing 20 trades an hour out of boredom or frustration will drain your account. Quality signals beat quantity every time.

❌
Wrong Expiry Times

If you're reading a 5m chart on SignalDesk, don't set a 30-second expiry on Pocket Option! Match your expiry to the signal timeframe.

❌
Chasing Runaway Prices

If price has already spiked 15 pips in the direction of the signal, wait for a minor pullback to the entry zone before entering.
